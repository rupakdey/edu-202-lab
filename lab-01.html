Connect to the Virtual Lab

Join the CloudShare session, locate the lab VMs and credentials, and open the Experience Center.

2 tasks Approx. 10 minutes Dedicated tenant
Lab objectives

What you will accomplish

  • Join using the instructor-shared CloudShare link and passphrase.
  • Use CloudShare clipboard controls and find the assigned tenant credentials.
  • Sign in to the Experience Center and locate features with Search Menu.
1.1

Open Your CloudShare Environment

Join the session and prepare your lab workspace.

  1. Open the CloudShare link displayed or shared by your instructor. Join with your corporate/work email and the instructor-provided passphrase. Free personal accounts such as Gmail or Yahoo are not accepted.
  2. Wait for the environment to become ready. Select Go to My VM List or VM List, then open Corp Client PC 1.
  3. To transfer text into a VM, choose Actions → Send Text, paste the text, then select Send.
  4. Use Lab Guide for course materials. Open Credentials in the left panel for your tenant and SDC accounts. No invitation or separate credential email is sent.
✓
Expected result

Your CloudShare workspace opens, the lab VM is available, and you can locate the tenant and SDC credential sections.

1.2

Sign In to the Experience Center

Use the assigned tenant administrator account from the lab workstation.

  1. In Corp Client PC 1, use Actions → Send Password to unlock Windows. The local username is student; the documented lab default is Admin-123!.
  2. Open https://console.zscaler.com ↗. Sign in with the administrator account and password shown in CloudShare → Credentials.

  3. Explore the available services. Use Search Menu to find the features named in the following labs.
✓
Expected result

The Experience Center opens for your assigned tenant and Search Menu is available.
