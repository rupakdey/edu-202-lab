Lab 1 · Hands-on

Connect to the Virtual Lab

Connect to your Skytap environment, start the lab systems, and sign in to the Zscaler Experience Center. You will also become familiar with the Skytap credential helper used throughout the course.

2 tasksApprox. 10 minutes
Lab objectives

What you will accomplish

  • Connect to Skytap and start all VMs.
  • Use the Skytap sign-in and credential helper.
  • Sign in to the Experience Center with your session-specific Zscaler administrator account.
1.1

Test Your Lab Access and Start Your Environment

Open your assigned Skytap pod and confirm the lab systems are ready.

  1. Open the unique Skytap Portal URL supplied with your lab access information.
  2. Select all VMs and click Play if they are not already running.
  3. Wait for the lab systems to show Running. The environment starts systems in stages, so this can take a few minutes.
  1. Open a VM from the Skytap portal. In the VM toolbar, select the Keys / Credentials icon to view the stored username and password.
  2. Use the Insert Credentials control when you want Skytap to enter the stored credentials into the foreground sign-in screen.
✓
Expected result

The Skytap pod is accessible, the required lab systems are running, and you can open a VM and use the Skytap credential helper.

1.2

Sign In to the Experience Center

Sign in to Corp: Client PC 1 and authenticate to your assigned Zscaler tenant.

  1. Open Corp: Client PC 1. Use the Skytap toolbar to send Ctrl-Alt-Del, then open the Keys / Credentials panel and insert the local student credentials.
  1. Open a browser and go to https://console.zscaler.com.
  2. Sign in with your session-specific Zscaler administrator account:
    Usernamestudent@<Student FQDN>
    PasswordUse the password supplied with your lab access information.

    <Student FQDN> is your assigned lab domain. Example: if the FQDN is zs000834.labtenant.com, the login is student@zs000834.labtenant.com.

  1. Confirm that the Experience Center loads successfully.
  1. Use Search menu when you need to locate a feature quickly. This is especially useful when a source-guide screenshot shows the older menu layout.
✓
Expected result

You are signed in to Corp: Client PC 1 and the Zscaler Experience Center for your assigned tenant is open and ready for the remaining labs.

✓
Lab 1 complete

You have completed the activities defined for Connect to the Virtual Lab.
