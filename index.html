Your session

Access your lab through CloudShare

Your instructor provides the CloudShare access link and passphrase to join the session. No invitation or separate credential emails are sent.

!
Use your corporate email address

Join CloudShare with your corporate/work email address and the instructor-provided passphrase. Free personal email accounts such as Gmail and Yahoo are not accepted.

Your assigned Zscaler tenant

After joining, open the Credentials panel in CloudShare. Use the tenant URL, administrator account, and password assigned to you. Sample values shown in this guide are illustrative only.

The CloudShare join passphrase and Zscaler tenant password serve different purposes. Use each only at its corresponding sign-in screen.

Your lab workspace

Use VM List to locate the virtual machines and Lab Guide for course materials. VM names and login methods are course-specific; follow the first lab.

If the course includes Solutions Demo Center (SDC) labs, find those accounts in the same CloudShare Credentials panel. SDC uses a separate environment; follow the checkpoint immediately before its first lab.
