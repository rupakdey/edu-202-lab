Checkpoint · Before Lab 10

Sign in to the Solutions Demo Center

Use the separate Solutions Demo Center environment to inspect existing ZDX reports and DLP workflows. CloudShare Credentials contains the assigned SDC identities.

Applies to: Labs 10–11 Access: Read-only review / instructor-guided actions Environment: Pre-built
!
Do not use your learner tenant for Labs 10–11

The Solutions Demo Center is separate from the tenant used during the earlier hands-on labs. Use the SDC credentials from CloudShare → Credentials when you reach this checkpoint.

What you need

Use the SDC credentials from CloudShare

Open CloudShare → Credentials and find the SDC section. No separate credential email is sent. Use the SDC administrator account for the portal and the SDC IT department account only when Lab 11.1 asks you to enroll ZCC. Complete 2FA enrollment if prompted.

Use these fields

  1. Admin User Login URL — open the administrator SDC sign-in page supplied in CloudShare → Credentials.
  2. Admin Username — use the assigned SDC administrator account.
  3. Password — use the SDC password shown in the same credential block.
  4. Complete 2FA enrollment if the environment prompts for it.
i
Other identities

Additional non-admin identities may be listed for demonstration scenarios. Use them only when a specific lab step instructs you to do so.

Different lab model

Analyze an existing environment

Use existing SDC reports and incidents. Lab 10 is read-only. Lab 11 includes source-described update and notification actions; inspect or follow the instructor demonstration when the assigned account cannot save or send.

Lab 10 · ZDX reports

Read-only analysis of existing views, reports and snapshots.

Lab 11 · Incident workflows

Inspect incidents and workflow mappings. Perform updates or notifications only when your instructor permits them and the account allows them.
